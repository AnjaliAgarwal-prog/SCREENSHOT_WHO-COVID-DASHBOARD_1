# PROJECT TITLE: "WHO-COVID-DASHBOARD".

# PURPOSE: Have a quick look on COVID REPORT given by WHO for years 2020,2021,2022 and 2023 via Dashboard.

# Technology: Microsoft Power BI.

# Data Source: Extracted from Search Engines. Then transformed according to requirement of columns to make the Dashboard.

# Problem: Takes too much time to analyze the data and extract the required information by Government and Hospitals given by "WHO" in tabular form.

# Goal of Dashboard: To represent the data published by WHO regarding "COVID" in one sight using different Visuals, Charts, buttons, Slicers etc. in single platform and give required information in quick and easy way.

# Key Visuals: In this Dashboard, I designed three pages, in which we can navigate by pressing ctrl + Action button.

# In Page1: known as Home page I represent, 

Total Cumulative cases, Total Cumulative Deaths and Total Vaccinations using Card Visuals, Sum of Cumulative cases and Sum of Cumulative Deaths by years and Months using Line Chart, Count of Who_region by Who_rgion using Pie Chart, A slicer to represent different countries and extract information only for required country, Finally, to action buttons are used to navigate in page2(Vaccine Dose) or page3(Vaccinations).

# In page2 : I Describe, 

The vaccine dose given to Sum of persons, Sum of persons vaccinated by 1 plus dose by years and months, Sum of persons vaccinated by BoosterAdd Dose by Years and months, Sum of persons vaccinated by Last Dose by Years and months using stacked bar charts, Again Slicer is added to select the country , Further an Action button is placed to navigate to Home page.

# In Page 3: I had done,

Representation of Total Vaccinations by Years and Months using Stacked Bar Chart, Count of Vaccines types used using pie Chart, Again slicer and an Action button is added .

# Impact and Insights:

 # Takes much less time to analyze the data and extract the required information by Government and Hospitals given by "WHO" in bulk, in single sight.
